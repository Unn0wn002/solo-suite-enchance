# Product discovery

Use the `planning` profile.

1. Gather the user, problem, evidence, constraints, desired outcome, and explicit non-goals.
2. Separate known facts from assumptions and identify the smallest validation needed.
3. Build a user-journey backbone, slice it into independently valuable releases, and rank by evidence, risk, and dependency.
4. Write measurable acceptance criteria and decision questions for design, architecture, QA, and analytics.
5. Do not invent market evidence or authorize implementation from unresolved high-risk assumptions.

Output a concise brief, prioritized story map, acceptance criteria, risks, and a handoff to UI/UX Design or Software Architecture. Verify that every proposed item traces to an outcome.
